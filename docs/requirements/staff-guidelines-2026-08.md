## 1. Attendance & Punctuality

- **SG-1.1** All staff members must report to the clinic by 9:45 AM every day.
- **SG-1.2** The second-floor clinic should be opened on time each morning.
- **SG-1.3** Staff should remain punctual and maintain professional discipline at all times.
- **SG-1.4** if anyone wants to take leave it should be pre- inform
- **SG-1.5** If leave is required, a written application must be submitted and approved at least one week in advance whenever possible.
- **SG-1.6** if u take leave on Saturday and Monday then Sunday will be considerate.

## 2. Clinic Cleanliness & Maintenance

- **SG-2.1** Ensure all treatment rooms, chairs, slabs, mirrors, and common areas are cleaned regularly.
- **SG-2.2** Dining and staff-use areas must be kept neat and hygienic.
- **SG-2.3** Chairs, doors, handles, washbasins, faucets, bathrooms, electrical switches, extension boards, and clinic furniture should be checked periodically for any maintenance issues.
- **SG-2.4** Report plumbing, electrical, or repair requirements immediately.
- **SG-2.5** Air conditioners should be maintained at the designated temperature (24°C) and switched off when rooms are unoccupied.
- **SG-2.6** Turn off unnecessary lights, fans, and equipment to avoid wastage.
- **SG-2.7** Water plants as scheduled.
- **SG-2.8** Monitor the water pump and switch it off once the water tank is full.
- **SG-2.9** air diffuser on or off check respected time.

## 3. Infection Control & Sterilization

- **SG-3.1** Ensure instruments are cleaned, sterilized, autoclaved, and stored properly.
- **SG-3.2** Use ultrasonic cleaners and UV chambers according to protocol.
- **SG-3.3** Bio-medical waste must be disposed of appropriately and on time.
- **SG-3.4** Replace black waste disposal bags regularly.
- **SG-3.5** Hand gloves, tissues, soap, liquid soap, hand wash, toilet paper, glasses, RVG sleeves, and other consumables should always be adequately stocked in every room.
- **SG-3.6** Both doctors and assistants must wear gloves during patient examinations and treatments, following infection control protocols.
- **SG-3.7** instrument well packed and sealed
- **SG-3.8** same day instruments should be autoclave and ultrasonic clean

## 4. Patient Care & Clinical Operations

- **SG-4.1** First thing in the morning all patients should confirm then after confirmation remaining information regarding the patient should confirm like remainder call and follow up
- **SG-4.2** Ensure all patients receive professional care and attention throughout their visit. And call too .
- **SG-4.3** Take and properly document pre-treatment and post-treatment scan for every patient.
- **SG-4.4** Provide clear pre-operative and post-operative instructions to all surgical patients.
- **SG-4.5** never forgot for consent form sign rct , surgery, facial aesthetic and implant etc.
- **SG-4.6** Conduct follow-up calls for surgery patients the day after the procedure and continue follow-ups as required.
- **SG-4.7** before surgery pt should be the remainder for medicine and c heavy meal on surgery day
- **SG-4.8** Monitor patient satisfaction and treatment progress through regular follow-up.
- **SG-4.9** after completed treatment ask for review on Google
- **SG-4.10** Before scheduling a crown delivery appointment, confirm that the crown has arrived at the clinic.
- **SG-4.11** Explain charitable services and provide relevant information brochures to eligible patients.
- **SG-4.12** Remain alert and prepared to respond appropriately to medical emergencies.
- **SG-4.13** cross check lab work received, send an appointment given to respected patients or not.

## 5. Appointment & Records Management

- **SG-5.1** Review and manage patient appointments daily.
- **SG-5.2** Maintain accurate and updated patient records and clinic documentation.
- **SG-5.3** Ensure all consent forms are properly completed and signed before any applicable procedure.
- **SG-5.4** Maintain proper laboratory records and documentation.

## 6. Laboratory Management

- **SG-6.1** Monitor all laboratory work and ensure timely completion.
- **SG-6.2** Maintain proper tracking and follow-up of laboratory cases.
- **SG-6.3** Verify that all laboratory materials and cases are organized and managed efficiently.

## 7. Inventory & Stock Management

- **SG-7.1** Regularly check clinic inventory and maintain adequate stock levels.
- **SG-7.2** Monitor implant systems and ensure all implant brands, components, and accessories are available and updated.
- **SG-7.3** Refill gloves, tissues, soap, hand wash, gauze, cotton rolls, and other consumables as required.
- **SG-7.4** Avoid unnecessary wastage of materials and resources.

## 8. Reception & Administrative Support

- **SG-8.1** Assist the reception team during free time when required.
- **SG-8.2** Help manage patient appointments, communication, and coordination.
- **SG-8.3** Ensure clinic phones and important reception equipment are handled responsibly.
- **SG-8.4** Monitor the reception area and ensure clinic property and materials are not removed without authorization.

## 9. Staff Conduct & Professionalism

- **SG-9.1** Maintain respectful, professional, and cooperative behavior at all times.
- **SG-9.2** Effective communication between doctors, assistants, reception staff, and management is mandatory.
- **SG-9.3** Arguments, conflicts, ego-related issues, or unprofessional behavior must never occur in front of patients.
- **SG-9.4** Follow assigned responsibilities carefully and complete all tasks accurately.
- **SG-9.5** Staff should not remain seated unnecessarily in the reception area during working hours.

## 10. Clinic Workflow & Coordination

- **SG-10.1** If there are no patients on the first floor, second-floor patients may be managed on the first floor to improve workflow efficiency.
- **SG-10.2** Staff should take lunch breaks in a coordinated manner to ensure uninterrupted patient care.
- **SG-10.3** A fixed lunch schedule should be followed by all team members.
- **SG-10.4** Ensure all departments remain informed and coordinated throughout the day.

## 11. Safety & Equipment Management

- **SG-11.1** Ensure all patient chairs and equipment remain functional and in good condition.
- **SG-11.2** Verify that file boxes, cabinets, and storage systems are intact and properly maintained.
- **SG-11.3** All assistants and doctors should be trained to operate the OPG X-ray machine to avoid dependency on limited personnel.

## 12. Workplace Standards

- **SG-12.1** Shoes must never be placed on patient or clinic chairs.
- **SG-12.2** Handle clinic equipment and instruments carefully and responsibly.
- **SG-12.3** Maintain a clean, organized, and professional clinical environment at all times.
- **SG-12.4** Support fellow staff members whenever assistance is needed to ensure smooth clinic operations.

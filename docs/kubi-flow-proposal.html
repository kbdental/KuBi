What changed, and why
  • One screen. No dashboard that repeats the other screens in different words.
  • No control numbers on the face of it. CLN-004 and OPEN-012 still run underneath — they surface as a sentence about a person, at the moment they block something, and never as a row to tick.
  • No percentage. Twenty-seven per cent ready did not tell anybody what to do; “the first patient is at 11:00 and two chairs are not set up” does.
  • Needs you is capped at three. Everything else waits its turn rather than being listed at you.
  • Move the clock and the whole screen moves. That is the thing a checklist could never show.
